# Your talk, as a website
layout: cover
subtitle: This example shows every layout. Replace it with your own talk.

Hi everyone. This is an example talk. Everything you see on these slides comes from one file, talk.md.

Press the right arrow to move on, or click anywhere on the slide. Press N to open the speaker notes.

---

# One file holds the whole talk
layout: split
image: sample-picture.svg
text: Each slide is a short block in talk.md. The words under the block are what the speaker says.

Every slide has two parts. At the top, a few short lines describe what the slide shows. Under them, after an empty line, comes everything I want to say out loud.

That spoken part shows up here, in the notes window. And when I fix a sentence in the notes while I rehearse, it saves straight back into talk.md.

---

# A slide works best with one idea
layout: statement

People can hold only a few things in mind at once. So each slide carries one idea, and the speaker says the rest.

---

# Lists can grow one point per click
layout: list
reveal: yes
points:
- The first point appears
- Then the second, on the next click
- Every point gets its own moment

With "reveal: yes", a list grows by one point with every click. The points already on screen stay exactly where they are, so the eye goes straight to the new one.

---

# Pictures can fill the whole slide
layout: image
image: sample-picture.svg
caption: A full picture, with a caption on top.

A picture can take the whole slide. The title and the caption sit on top of it.

---

layout: quote
quote: The best slides leave room for the speaker.
by: Someone wise

A quote gets its own slide, in large type, with the name below it.

---

# Two sides, side by side
layout: compare
left: Before — A wall of bullet points on every slide.
right: After — One idea per slide, and the speaker says the rest.

Compare puts two ideas next to each other. The first card is light and the second one is dark, so the difference is easy to see.

---

# Code looks like code
layout: code
lang: javascript
code:
```
function greet(name) {
  return `Hello, ${name}!`;
}

greet("everyone");
```

Code sits in a dark window, in a monospace font. Short snippets read best: ten lines or fewer.

---

# Numbers turn into a chart
layout: chart
text: A bar chart from a few lines of text.
bars:
- Slides with one idea: 82
- Slides with three ideas: 46
- Slides with ten bullet points: 12

Each line under "bars" becomes one bar: a label, a colon, and a number. These numbers are made up for the example.

---

# Videos play on their own
layout: video
video: sample-clip.mp4

A video fills the slide, plays without sound, and loops. Clips from a screen recording work well here.

---

One big number can carry a whole slide. Use it for the one fact people should remember.
