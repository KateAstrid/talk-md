# My presentation style

Copy this file to `STYLE.md` and replace the examples with your own preferences. Your AI agent follows `STYLE.md` for every slide and every line of your speaker notes.

## Tone

- Example: friendly and relaxed, like talking to colleagues over coffee.
- Example: a little humour is welcome; no jokes in the closing.

## Words

- Example: say "customers", not "users".
- Example: avoid jargon such as "synergy" and "leverage".

## Sentences

- Example: short sentences that are easy to say out loud.
- Example: speak as "I" when telling my own stories.

## Slides

- Example: always use the dark theme.
- Example: end every talk with a slide that shows my website.
